The website and catalog are yours. Your photos, your logo, your business content stay yours. You own your domain name. I'm providing the build and the hosting.

If you need a domain name, I'll register it for you. You own it. No auto-renewal without your say-so.

Your logo, clear photos of your work, and your business details (phone, email, service area, whatever you want on the site). The build can't start until these come in.

If you decide not to move forward before the build starts, you get your deposit back and we're square. If you walk after the build is underway, the deposit stays as payment for the work done. If I can't complete the work for any reason and haven't delivered, I refund what you've paid.
